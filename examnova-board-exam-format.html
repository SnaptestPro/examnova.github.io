General Instructions
    Section A: Objective Type Questions 1 x 10 = 10 Marks
    1. Choose the correct answer from the given options. 1
    2. Fill in the blank with the correct answer. 1
    3. State whether the given statement is true or false. 1
    4. Answer the following in one word or one sentence. 1
    Section B: Very Short Answer Questions 2 x 5 = 10 Marks
    1. Answer the following question briefly. 2
    2. Solve the given problem and write the final answer. 2
    3. Define the given term with one suitable example. 2
    Section C: Short Answer Questions 3 x 6 = 18 Marks
    1. Attempt the question with proper steps. 3
    2. Explain the concept with a neat example. 3
    3. Write a short note on the given topic. 3
    Section D: Long Answer Questions 5 x 4 = 20 Marks
    1. Solve the following question in detail. 5
    2. Answer the question with proper explanation and diagram if required. 5
      3. OR
    3. Attempt the alternative question in detail. 5
    Section E: Case / Source Based Questions 4 x 3 = 12 Marks
    1. Read the given passage or case and answer the sub-questions. 4
    2. Observe the data, diagram, map, or table and answer. 4
